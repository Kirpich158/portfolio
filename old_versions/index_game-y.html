I'm a game developer focused on playable ads — small, mobile-first games built for ad placements, rewarded videos, and organic reach. I've shipped playables for brands and studios, taking each one from concept and prototype to a shippable build that hits frame budget and conversion targets.

When I'm not squeezing a game into a 4 MB budget, I care deeply about the details: juicy feedback, clean art, and games that feel great even muted, on a cramped screen, on a low-end device. My process is simple — prototype fast, playtest honestly, polish relentlessly.
