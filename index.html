Chapter 2 — About Me

Why I do this

Everytime it is a facinating challenge to squeeze maximum from a mobile hardware and a time limit of playables, to give players engaging experience, but also the one that feels good. Every detail, every animation matters for the overall satisfaction of player and potential clicks towards the download button.

I've spent the last few years making playable ads for different games and studios. It's a fun niche — you have to make something that feels like a real game, but it also has to sell another game, a demo of some sorts.

By nature, I care about the small details, in my opinion they are the ones making playable thrive, they give it that finished, polished feel. Every action, every interaction must give player a feedback, kinda of Nintendo way :D My approach is pretty simple: details and satisfaction, as fast as possible.
